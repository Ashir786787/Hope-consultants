## Destinations

1. New Zealand — NZQF accredited qualifications, strong post-study work rights, and a clear pathway to residence. Study once, settle for good.
2. Australia — World-class universities, summer-style living, and generous post-study work rights with a range of PR pathway options.
3. Canada — Semester-based admissions, strong work rights for students, and one of the most student-friendly PR systems in the world.
4. United Kingdom — Shorter one-year master's programmes, a global academic reputation, and a strong graduate route for work after study.
5. Ireland — Home to Europe's tech giants, moderate tuition, and a two-year post-study work visa that many students convert into residence.
6. Germany — Tuition-free education at public universities, a booming job market, and an 18-month post-study work visa.
7. France — World-class engineering and business schools, affordable tuition at public universities, and a clear post-study work pathway.
8. Poland — Lower tuition and living costs, growing global recognition of degrees, and strong post-study work options.
9. Malaysia — Affordable degrees with international campuses, booming tech hubs, and a low cost of living for students.
10. USA — The widest range of universities on earth, powerful alumni networks, and STEM pathways to long-term work and residence.
11. Denmark — Free tuition for many EU students, English-taught degrees, and a top-ranked tech and design ecosystem.
12. Netherlands — English-first country with internationally ranked universities and a culture built for international graduates.
13. Norway — Free public university tuition, a purely English-friendly teaching environment, and one of the best quality-of-life records on earth.
14. Sweden — Innovation and design capital of the north, English-taught programmes, and a green, sustainable lifestyle.
