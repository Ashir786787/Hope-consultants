# 04 — Scholarships

Eyebrow: Scholarships
h2: Find the money that actually exists
Paragraph: Real scholarships and grants, matched to your profile and budget. We update this list monthly and never add a scheme we have not verified.

## Scholarships

1. Name: New Zealand International Scholarships
   Group: Government-funded
   Country: New Zealand
   Covers: Full tuition + living allowance
   Advice: Highly competitive and applications open once a year. Start on the documents at least six months before the intake you are targeting.
   Featured: true

2. Name: Australia Awards Scholarships
   Group: Government-funded
   Country: Australia
   Covers: Full tuition + travel + living allowance
   Advice: For students with strong academic records and a clear intention to contribute to Pakistan on return. Attendance at workshops is expected.
   Featured: true

3. Name: Commonwealth Scholarships (UK)
   Group: Government-funded
   Country: United Kingdom
   Covers: Full tuition + stipend + flights
   Advice: Split into taught and research categories. Academic references matter as much as grades — start on them early.
   Featured: false

4. Name: DAAD Scholarships (Germany)
   Group: Provider-funded
   Country: Germany
   Covers: Monthly stipend, partial
   Advice: Most DAAD schemes require you to already hold an offer from a German university. Apply to universities and DAAD in parallel.
   Featured: false

5. Name: Eiffel Excellence Scholarships (France)
   Group: Provider-funded
   Country: France
   Covers: Monthly stipend + some travel
   Advice: Only for applications submitted through a French partner university. Confirm the partnership before applying.
   Featured: false

6. Name: Ireland Government Scholarships
   Group: Government-funded
   Country: Ireland
   Covers: Partial tuition
   Advice: Good for postgraduate research candidates. The proposal quality matters more than the GPA.
   Featured: false

7. Name: Malaysian Government Scholarships
   Group: Government-funded
   Country: Malaysia
   Covers: Tuition + living costs
   Advice: Applications roll on specific intakes. Language requirements are separate from the scholarship application, so track both.
   Featured: false

8. Name: Canada: IDRC Research Awards
   Group: Provider-funded
   Country: Canada
   Covers: Stipend for research periods
   Advice: For research students at the master's level. You need a supervisor willing to host you before you can apply.
   Featured: false

9. Name: USA: Fulbright Foreign Student Program
   Group: Government-funded
   Country: USA
   Covers: Full tuition + stipend + flights
   Advice: The strongest brand in scholarships, and the most competitive. Essay quality and genuine community commitment decide the outcome.
   Featured: false

10. Name: Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters
    Group: Provider-funded
    Country: Europe (multi-country)
    Covers: Full tuition + monthly stipend
    Advice: Courses span multiple countries. A single application serves several host universities. Check the stats of each course you apply to.
    Featured: false

11. Name: Poland: NAWA Scholarships
    Group: Government-funded
    Country: Poland
    Covers: Stipend + partial tuition
    Advice: Open to specific subject areas; availability changes each round. We check the current call list before advising.
    Featured: false

12. Name: Denmark: Danish Government Scholarships
    Group: Government-funded
    Country: Denmark
    Covers: Partial tuition waivers + stipend
    Advice: Awarded by individual universities rather than the state. You must apply to the university first and be nominated by them.
    Featured: false

13. Name: Netherlands: Orange Knowledge Programme
    Group: Provider-funded
    Country: Netherlands
    Covers: Full tuition + stipend
    Advice: Country and priority area quotas change each round. Eligibility is checked against the latest call text, not last year's.
    Featured: false

14. Name: Norway: Quota Scheme
    Group: Government-funded
    Country: Norway
    Covers: Full tuition, needs-based
    Advice: Ceased for new international students; only renewing students remain eligible. We flag current status honestly.
    Featured: false

15. Name: Sweden: Swedish Institute Scholarships
    Group: Government-funded
    Country: Sweden
    Covers: Full tuition + stipend
    Advice: For master's programmes. Apply via the university admissions portal first, then to the Swedish Institute.
    Featured: false
